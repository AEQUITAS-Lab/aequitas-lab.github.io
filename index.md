# **A**dvancing **EQUI**table **T**echnology & **A**lgorithms in **S**ociety

AEQUITAS Lab is a research lab situated in [Teachers College, Columbia University](https://www.tc.columbia.edu/). Directed by Dr. [Renzhe Yu](https://renzheyu.com), we conduct sociotechnical research to inform the design, deployment, and evaluation of emerging technologies and algorithms in education and society. We partner with under-resourced educational institutions and marginalized populations to help improve their conditions with responsible data science and AI.
